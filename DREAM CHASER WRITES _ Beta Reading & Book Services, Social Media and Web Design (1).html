Frequently asked questions

What services do you offer?

Beta reading and book services, social media management, and website design and development.

What kinds of websites can you design?

Personal, business, event, author, nonprofit, blog and ecommerce websites, among others.

Which platforms do you build on?

Any CMS platform. Tell us what you need and we will work with it.

How do I start a project?

Use the form below, or message us on WhatsApp or by email, and tell us which service you need.

What information do you need to start?

Tell us which service you need and what you want to achieve. It also helps to share your manuscript's genre, your brand and audience, or examples of websites and accounts you like.

Which genres do you beta read?

Fiction and nonfiction across romance, fantasy, sci-fi, mystery, thriller, horror, historical fiction, literary fiction, YA, contemporary, crime, adventure, paranormal and more. Cross-genre books are welcome.

How long will my project take?

It depends on the service and the size of the project. Tell us what you need and we will discuss a timeline together.
